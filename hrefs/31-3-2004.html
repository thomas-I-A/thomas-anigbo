31-3-2004


This is okay to be said to be the worst day of my entire life.

As a kid growing up i had everthing i could ever ask of but most importantly i had a dad who loved us (me and my siblings ) with all he had even with his own life.

I grew up being loved and knew only one man as father, my dad. I was always seen beside him most of the time cos besides being a dad he was a friend, a methor, my hero and my run to when mom raised a cane, but some how it all ended so soon. It all started when my mom received a call that my dad was hospitalised.

My dad was a very hard working ,dedicated, and disciplined business man who had businesses in several states in nigeria as a whole. So one faithful morning he had to travel for a business outside of lagos cos we lived in lagos state at the time but i could hear my younger brother telling him, "daddy don't travel" but he insisted since it was important. Few days after my mom received a call that my dad was hospitalised. She hurriedly packed her neccessary belongings and our's and we took the next available transport by road and went to the eastern part of nigeria, cos he had plans of traveling through some states but when he got to enugu (east of nigeria) was when we received the emergency call that dad is in the hospital. My dad had a partial stroke.

We got back to lagos. Seeing my dad he couldn't do all the things he use to and very often he alwaays had an attack (cerebrovascular accident (CVA)). It really hurts havong to see him go through all that pain. My mom didn't stop praying and taking him to hospitals for treatment. Some days it got better and some days we didin't know what else to do. This went on for three years. My dad had stroke for three years. Now i watched the man who was everything to us barely be fully a part of our lives. I miss his smiles, he's a man of few words, loves his space and always in his thoughts.

After some years passed on he could no longer accompany my mom on the business trips to show and direct her on how to run the business so most times he stayed behind and let her take care of the business and trips. On this day my mom went on a business trip and it was just me, my siblings and my dad. I happened to be awake playing video game at the time, it was mid-night so everyone was asleep but me. I was quite addictive to the video game that most nights i denied myself some sleep just so i can spend the night playing video games. That mid-night i was awake focused on my video game but also i mostly checked on my dad to see how he is doing and if he needed anything but most times he was sleeping deeply. Not until some few minutes passed my elder sister woke up and went to check on him when when she shouted, "dad's not breathing".
